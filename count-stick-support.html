COUNT STICK

A clearer way to count.
拍一张，数清楚。

Photograph your sticks or skewers, review the markers and keep a count you can check.

Getting started

  1. Place sticks against a plain, contrasting background with their ends visible. Use even lighting and avoid blur or heavy overlap.
  2. Choose Camera or Photos. Automatic counting runs on your device.
  3. Review every marker. Add missed items, remove extra marks and undo corrections as needed.
  4. Save a local record with an optional note, or share the result from the system share sheet.

Automatic results are estimates. Lighting, overlap, object size and image quality can affect them. Always verify a count before using it for a bill, inventory decision or other important purpose.

Troubleshooting

Camera is unavailable

Check camera permission in iOS Settings. On a device without a camera, use Photos.

Nothing was detected or the count is wrong

Try a closer, well-lit photo with clearly separated stick ends. You can correct the markers manually. Count Stick is designed for sticks and skewers; it is not a general-purpose object counter.

Where are my saved counts?

Open History on the same device. Records are local and are not synchronized between devices. Export anything you want to keep before uninstalling the app.

使用帮助

  1. 选择与签子颜色有对比的背景，让签头清晰可见，保持光线均匀，避免模糊和严重遮挡。
  2. 点击拍照或选择照片。自动识别在设备本地完成。
  3. 逐个检查标记；补上漏计、删除误计，必要时撤销修正。
  4. 添加备注并保存本地记录，或通过系统分享菜单分享结果。

自动计数是估算结果，受光线、遮挡、物体大小和照片质量影响。用于结账、库存或其他重要用途前，请核对每个标记。

相机无法打开

请在 iOS 设置中检查相机权限。没有相机的设备可以使用照片选择功能。

识别为零或数量不对

请靠近拍摄，让签头分开、清晰并有足够光线，也可以手动修正标记。App 面向签子和竹签，不承诺识别所有物体。
