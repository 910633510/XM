CountScope helps you review photo counts. The current automatic mode focuses on the exposed ends of a bundle of sticks or skewers.

Getting started

  1. Point the camera at the visible ends of a bundle. Use even lighting, a contrasting background and a sharp image.
  2. Choose Camera or Photos. Automatic counting and the reference-area estimate run on your device.
  3. Check every visible end, inside and outside the dashed reference area. Add missed markers, remove extra marks and undo corrections as needed.
  4. Save a local record with an optional note, or share a marked result from the system share sheet.

Automatic results are estimates. Lighting, overlap, object size and image quality can affect them. Always verify a count before using it for a bill, inventory decision or other important purpose. This version does not provide general-purpose object recognition.

Free counts and subscriptions

Four free automatic counts

CountScope is free to download. You can complete 4 automatic photo counts without subscribing. This is a one-time allowance on the current device, not a daily allowance. A completed automatic result, including zero detected markers, uses one count. Failed or canceled processing, manual corrections and reopening saved results do not use an additional free count. The used allowance is kept in the device's Keychain, survives app restarts and may remain after reinstalling the app. It is not synced between devices.

CountScope Pro monthly subscription

An active CountScope Pro subscription enables unlimited automatic photo counts. Eligible first-time subscribers can start a 7-day free trial. When the trial ends, the subscription renews monthly at the price Apple displays before confirmation. The U.S. price is US$2.99 per month; other storefronts display their local Apple price. Apple determines introductory-offer eligibility, with one introductory offer per subscription group. A new installation does not necessarily make an existing subscriber eligible again.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account after purchase confirmation, or at the end of an eligible free trial. The subscription automatically renews unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Renewal is charged within the 24 hours before the end of that period. The purchase screen shows the applicable price, trial eligibility and renewal terms before you confirm.

Restore or manage a subscription

Use Restore Purchases in the app with the same Apple Account that made the purchase. Purchase and restoration require internet access. To manage or cancel, open Apple Account subscriptions or use the app's subscription management control. Canceling or allowing a subscription to expire does not delete saved records. Refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Troubleshooting

Camera is unavailable

Check camera permission in iOS Settings. On a device without a camera, use Photos.

Nothing was detected or the count is wrong

Try a closer, well-lit photo with clear stick ends. Correct the markers manually and check the whole photo; the automatic reference outline can exclude missed ends.

Where are my saved counts?

Open History on the same device. Records are local and are not synchronized between devices. Share or export anything you want to keep before uninstalling the app.

使用帮助

CountScope 帮你核对照片中的数量。当前自动识别模式面向一束竹签或木签露出的端头，不提供通用物体识别。

  1. 对准签束的可见端头，选择有对比的背景，保持光线均匀和画面清晰。
  2. 点击拍照或选择照片。自动识别和参考范围估计在设备本地完成。
  3. 检查虚线范围内外的每个签头；补加漏标、删除误标，必要时撤销修正。
  4. 添加备注并保存本地记录，或通过系统分享菜单分享结果。

自动计数是估算结果，受光线、遮挡、物体大小和照片质量影响。用于结账、库存或其他重要用途前，请逐点核对整张照片。

常见问题

相机无法打开

请在 iOS 设置中检查相机权限。没有相机的设备可以使用照片选择功能。

识别为零或数量不对

请靠近拍摄，让签头清晰并有足够光线，再手动修正标记。参考虚线范围外也可能有漏掉的签头，请检查整张照片。

本地历史记录

历史记录保存在当前设备，不跨设备同步。卸载前请分享导出需要保留的记录。
