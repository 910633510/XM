Web privacy notice

This notice applies to the browser workspace at www.xmaillc.com/audio-studio/. It describes the web account and creative-assistant flows, which differ from the native app. The existing native-app policy and company terms remain available from the links below.

Google sign-in and your account

Signing in with Google creates an XM LLC web account the first time you use it. We receive and verify your Google account identifier, basic profile and email address. The sign-in integration does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive or your contacts. Your Google password is not sent to XM LLC.

Our service uses a secure, HTTP-only session cookie to keep you signed in and a request token to protect account actions. Sign out ends the current session. Google operates its own sign-in services under its policies.

Audio and creative projects

You choose the recordings, songs, text and settings to submit. Microphone access starts only after you select recording and grant browser permission. You can upload a file instead. Only submit recordings, voices, music and lyrics you own or have permission to use.

XM LLC audio services process selected voice references, speech text, songs, lyrics and generation settings to create audio. The web service stores your uploaded voices, projects, job progress and saved creations under your account so they can be reopened in another browser after sign-in. Browser drafts and interface preferences may also be kept on the current device; those are not a backup of your web account.

OpenAI creative planning

Before you use AI draft generation or revision, the workspace asks you to agree to send your creative brief, script or lyrics, settings and voice names/identifiers to OpenAI. These requests use the OpenAI API to prepare a plan. This planning integration does not send the voice-reference recordings or source-song audio to OpenAI. The separate audio-generation services still process the audio needed for synthesis.

Membership and payments

Stripe hosts the web subscription checkout and billing portal. We use customer, subscription, price, payment-state and billing-event identifiers to verify web access and process payment changes. Payment-card details are entered on Stripe's checkout, not stored by the audio workspace. Web purchases and Apple in-app purchases are separate; Google sign-in does not automatically link an Apple subscription or synchronize native-app files.

Storage, deletion and service records

Saved web projects, uploads and creations remain available until you remove them or delete the web account. Deleting a workspace item removes the corresponding item from the web workspace. Account deletion revokes web sessions and removes that account's workspace data. Exported downloads, payment records, backups and processing copies in separate generation services are not all automatically erased by these actions.

Stopping or pausing a job does not guarantee that an already-submitted upstream generation stops immediately. Operational logs, anti-abuse records and billing-event records may remain for service operation and payment handling. For help locating or deleting processing copies, contact XM LLC support with the feature, approximate time and job identifier. Do not send passwords, session cookies or API keys.

Requests use HTTPS in production. Server access controls separate one web account's private files and jobs from another. These controls do not mean that the underlying content is anonymous or end-to-end encrypted.

Web membership

The account screen and Stripe checkout show the current web price, billing currency and monthly renewal details before purchase. A web membership enables the speech and full-cover benefits shown in the workspace. Music creation and AI planning follow the access state displayed by the service.

Subscriptions renew until cancelled. Use Manage subscription in the account area to manage or cancel through Stripe. Payment confirmation comes from the billing service; returning to a success page alone does not activate access. If access has not updated, refresh account status or contact support.

Deleting a recording or project does not cancel a subscription. Manage an active subscription before deleting the web account. Web membership does not automatically change your Apple subscription; manage Apple purchases through Apple.

Generation duration is a target and results can vary. Review generated scripts and audio before using or publishing them. The workspace provides downloads; it does not automatically post your creations elsewhere.

网页版隐私说明

本说明适用于 www.xmaillc.com/audio-studio/ 网页工作台。网页版使用 Google 账户和网页订阅，其数据流程与原生 App 不同。

账户与登录

首次使用 Google 登录时会创建 XM LLC 网页账户。服务端验证 Google 账户标识、基本个人资料和邮箱，不申请 Gmail、Google Drive 或通讯录权限，也不会接收你的 Google 密码。网站使用安全的 HTTP-only 会话 Cookie 和请求校验令牌；退出登录会结束当前会话。

创作内容与 AI 文稿

你主动选择要提交的录音、歌曲、文字和参数。只有点击录音并允许浏览器使用麦克风后才会开始录音，也可以直接上传文件。请仅使用自己拥有或已获授权的素材和声音。

声音克隆、配音、翻唱和音乐生成由 XM LLC 的音频服务处理。网页版按账户保存上传声音、项目、任务进度和作品，便于登录后重新打开。浏览器中的草稿和界面设置可能只保存在当前设备，不能视为账户备份。

使用 AI 生成或修改文稿前，页面会请你同意将创作需求、文稿或歌词、参数和音色名称/标识发送给 OpenAI。该规划流程不把参考声音录音或原始歌曲音频发送给 OpenAI；生成音频所需素材仍由相应音频服务处理。

订阅与支付

网页订阅通过 Stripe 的结账和账单页面处理。网站使用客户、订阅、价格及支付状态等记录验证权益，不保存你在 Stripe 输入的银行卡资料。购买前会显示实际价格、币种和月度续费信息。可在账户页面管理或取消订阅；支付完成后的网页跳转本身不会授予会员。

网页订阅与 Apple App 内购买相互独立。Google 登录不会自动关联 Apple 会员或同步原生 App 文件。删除作品不等于取消订阅；删除网页账户前请先管理有效订阅。

保存与删除

上传声音、项目和作品保留在网页账户中，直到你删除相关内容或账户。账户删除会撤销网页会话并移除该账户的工作台数据，但已下载的文件、支付记录、备份和独立生成服务中的处理副本不会因此全部自动删除。暂停或取消也不保证已经提交的上游生成立即停止。

如需查找或删除服务端处理副本，请联系 XM LLC 支持，提供功能、时间和任务标识；不要发送密码、Cookie 或 API 密钥。生产连接使用 HTTPS，服务端按账户隔离私有文件和任务；这不代表内容匿名或采用端到端加密。
