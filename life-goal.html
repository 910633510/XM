xsimple Life Goal

xsimple Life Goal, also localized as 人生小目标, helps people turn personal goals into realistic daily work using a private on-device AI planner.

  • Create a goal and optional completion date.
  • Review the AI-generated daily checklist and monthly calendar.
  • Mark daily work complete or rescale missed work over the time left.
  • AI planning runs locally on the device; goal content is not submitted to third-party AI services.
