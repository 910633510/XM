Privacy Policy

This policy explains how XM LLC handles information for the xSimple VPN apps and the account, subscription and VPN services that support them.

Read the policy Contact us

Updated October 22, 2026

Information used to provide the service

  • Account information. If you create an account, we process your email address, a password hash and account creation information to authenticate you and provide account access.
  • Device and access information. We process a hashed device identifier, account and device-session timestamps, access status and expiry. Device records support service access and trial-abuse prevention.
  • VPN configuration. Provisioning a connection uses tunnel configuration such as a public VPN key, an assigned tunnel IP address and the selected service location.
  • Purchase information. We process subscription status, expiry and purchase identifiers, including Apple's original transaction identifier, to validate and restore access.

VPN traffic and operational diagnostics

VPN servers process your network traffic and its destination addresses to provide the connection you request. Connection diagnostics can include source and destination IP addresses and timestamps.

Our service API records request time, HTTP method, API path, response status, client IP address, user-agent and request duration. Error diagnostics can also contain account identifiers, assigned tunnel addresses, service locations and error details.

We use these records to operate the service, investigate connection problems, validate access and protect the service against misuse.

Payments and service providers

On iPhone and iPad, Apple processes App Store purchases. We receive the purchase and entitlement information needed to provide access; Apple does not provide us with your payment-card details.

Where the Mac app offers web billing, checkout and subscription management use Stripe's hosted pages. Stripe processes the payment information you enter, and the service uses billing identifiers and subscription status to manage access.

The infrastructure providers that host our account and VPN services process data needed to deliver those services. Apple and Stripe also handle information under their own privacy policies: Apple Privacy Policy and Stripe Privacy Policy.

Storage and retention

We retain information for account and service operation, security, troubleshooting and entitlement validation. Account, device, purchase and operational records can have different retention needs. This policy does not specify a fixed automatic-deletion period.

A hashed device identifier can still be associated with your service access. We treat device and connection records as service information even when they do not contain your name.

Account deletion and your choices

You can use Delete Account while signed in to the app, or contact us at xm910llc@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion of your data. Please identify xSimple VPN and the account or device-related issue so we can locate the relevant records.

Account deletion removes the account, associated VPN peers and account sessions. Separate device, subscription and security records may remain after account deletion. Contact us about those records or if you used the service without an account.

Deleting your account or uninstalling the app does not cancel an Apple subscription. Manage or cancel an iOS subscription in your Apple Account subscription settings.

Contact and policy updates

XM LLC operates xSimple VPN. Send privacy questions and data requests to xm910llc@gmail.com. We will post changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.
