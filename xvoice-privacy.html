xVoice Clone Privacy Policy

xVoice Clone converts authorized voice samples into secure, policy-aware synthetic models. This document details the data lifecycle practices that keep customer recordings and resulting assets private throughout the engagement.

Jump to Policy Back to XM LLC

Effective: May 19, 2026

Data we collect

  • Text prompts, uploaded or recorded voice reference audio, and generated audio are collected only when you choose to submit them in the App.
  • Voice profile content you create in the App is used to provide voice cloning and text-to-speech functionality.
  • Purchase history and subscription status may be used to unlock paid features. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store.
  • We do not create user accounts.
  • We do not track you or use the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA).
  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not request access to Contacts, Photos, Camera, Microphone, Location, Health, or other sensitive data.

How we use data

User-provided text, audio, voice profile content, and generated audio are used for App functionality: creating AI voice output, saving reusable voice profiles, exporting generated audio, and supporting subscription access.

Third parties

xVoice Clone does not use a third-party AI service and does not send user text, voice reference audio, generated audio, or other user content to a third-party AI provider. Voice processing is handled by XM LLC's own backend service. The App may interact with Apple systems such as the App Store and StoreKit for app delivery and purchases. Apple processes that information under its own privacy policies.

Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the age required in your region). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Security

User-provided content is transmitted to XM LLC's backend over encrypted connections and is used only to provide the requested App functionality. We do not sell user data or use it for advertising.

Changes to this policy

If xVoice Clone adds features that collect additional data, such as analytics, crash logs, or login, we will update this policy and the App's disclosures before those changes go live.
