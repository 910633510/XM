Chain Rift Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

XM LLC provides Chain Rift, a single-player virtual reality game for Meta Quest.

Information used to play

The game uses headset and controller tracking and input during play to render your view and control movement and equipment. This information is processed on your headset. The game does not transmit it to XM LLC or third parties.

The game runs offline and has no in-game account, advertising, or analytics. Settings such as language, difficulty, turning, comfort vignette and your selected hook, along with tutorial and achievement progress, are stored in the app’s local data so the game can restore them.

Managing and deleting information

You can remove local game data by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it. Any backup or restore handled by Meta follows your headset settings and Meta’s privacy policy.

If you email us, we use your email address and the details you provide to respond. For privacy questions or to ask us to delete information you sent to support, email xm910llc@gmail.com.

Meta accounts, headset services and any system-level recording or sharing are covered by Meta’s own privacy policy.

锁链裂谷隐私政策

更新日期为 2026 年 11 月 18 日。

锁链裂谷是 XM LLC 提供的一款 Meta Quest 单机 VR 游戏。

游戏使用的信息

游戏使用头显和手柄的追踪信息及输入来呈现视角，并控制移动和装备。信息在头显上处理，游戏不会将其传给 XM LLC 或第三方。

游戏离线运行，没有游戏账号、广告或统计分析。语言、难度、转向方式、舒适遮罩、所选钩子、教学和成就进度等会保存在应用的本地数据中，供下次游玩时恢复。

管理和删除信息

清除应用数据或卸载游戏可删除本地游戏数据。由 Meta 提供的备份和恢复遵循头显设置及 Meta 的隐私政策。

如果你发邮件联系我们，我们会使用你的邮箱地址和提供的信息来回复。你可以发邮件至 xm910llc@gmail.com 咨询隐私问题，或请求删除你提供给客服的信息。

Meta 账号、头显服务以及系统的录制和分享功能适用 Meta 自己的隐私政策。
