COUNTSCOPE

Privacy Policy
隐私政策

Effective date: October 25, 2026 · 生效日期：2026年10月25日

Your photos stay on your device

CountScope is a photo-counting app provided by XM LLC. Its current automatic counting mode focuses on visible stick and skewer ends. Photo counting, text-region analysis and the reference-area estimate run on your device. Recognized text is not stored or transmitted. The app does not upload your photos or counting results to XM LLC or an AI provider.

Camera and photo selection

Camera access is used only when you choose to take a photo. You can deny or revoke access in iOS Settings and still import a photo. The system photo picker gives the app access to the images you select, without requiring access to your whole photo library.

Saved history

When you save a count, the app stores the photo, markers, total, date and optional note in its local storage. These records are excluded from the app's iCloud backup. You can delete individual records in History; uninstalling the app removes its local history. Records are not synced between devices.

Free-count allowance

The app provides 4 automatic photo counts without a subscription. To keep track of this one-time allowance, it stores the used-count state locally in the device's Keychain. This state is not sent to XM LLC and is not synchronized to another device. It survives app restarts and may remain after the app is uninstalled and reinstalled. It does not contain your photos, notes or counting results.

Apple subscriptions

CountScope uses Apple's StoreKit for its optional auto-renewable monthly subscription and eligible 7-day introductory free trial. Apple handles payment, trial eligibility, renewals, cancellations and restoration under Apple's Privacy Policy. The app obtains product and transaction information through StoreKit and verifies subscription entitlement on your device. Purchase and restoration require internet access. XM LLC does not operate a receipt-validation server for this app, does not receive your payment card details, and does not receive your in-app transaction history from the app.

You can manage or cancel your subscription in Apple Account subscription settings. Uninstalling the app does not cancel an Apple subscription. The subscription terms are explained on the support page and the purchase screen. Apple's Standard EULA applies.

Sharing

Sharing happens only when you choose Share and select a destination in the system share sheet. The selected app or recipient receives what you share under its own privacy practices.

No app account, advertising or tracking

CountScope does not require an app account and does not include advertising, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. XM LLC does not receive your in-app photos, counts or usage history. StoreKit's communication with Apple is used for purchases and subscription access, not for uploading your photos. App Store or operating-system diagnostics you elect to share are governed by Apple's settings and policies.

Support and policy changes

If you email us, we receive the message and details you choose to provide, and use them to respond to your request. Please avoid sending sensitive photos, Apple Account passwords or payment details. This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the app; the effective date will be updated on this page.

照片留在你的设备上

CountScope 是 XM LLC 提供的拍照计数工具，当前自动识别模式面向可见的竹签或木签端头。照片计数、文字区域分析和参考范围估计均在设备上进行，识别出的文字不会被保存或传输。App 不会将照片或计数结果上传给 XM LLC 或 AI 服务提供商。

相机与照片选择

只有在你选择拍照时，App 才会使用相机。你可以拒绝或在 iOS 设置中撤销权限，仍可选择照片。系统照片选择器仅向 App 提供你主动选中的照片，无需开放整个相册。

本地历史记录

当你主动保存计数时，照片、标记、数量、日期和可选备注会保存在 App 的本地存储中。这些记录不纳入 App 的 iCloud 备份。你可以在历史记录中逐条删除；卸载 App 会移除本地历史记录。记录不跨设备同步。

免费识别额度

未订阅时可使用4次自动识别。为记录这份一次性额度，App 会将已用次数保存在设备钥匙串中，不会发送给 XM LLC，也不会同步到其他设备。重启后额度记录仍会保留，卸载再安装后也可能保留。此额度记录不包含照片、备注或计数结果。

Apple 订阅

可选的自动续费月度订阅和符合资格的7天免费试用通过 Apple StoreKit 提供。付款、试用资格、续费、取消和恢复均由 Apple 按照其隐私政策处理。App 通过 StoreKit 获取商品与交易信息，并在设备本地验证订阅权益。购买与恢复需要联网。XM LLC 不为此 App 运营收据验证服务器，不会收到你的银行卡信息，也不会从 App 接收你的应用内交易历史。

可在 Apple 账户订阅设置中管理或取消订阅。卸载 App 不会取消 Apple 订阅。订阅条款见支持页面和购买界面；适用 Apple 标准 EULA。

分享

只有在你点击分享并在系统分享菜单中选择目标后，内容才会发给所选 App 或接收者。对方按其自身隐私规则处理这些内容。

无需 App 账号，无广告和追踪

CountScope 无需注册 App 账号，不包含广告、第三方分析或追踪 SDK。XM LLC 不会收到 App 内的照片、计数或使用记录。StoreKit 与 Apple 的通信仅用于购买和订阅权益，不会上传你的照片。你选择向 Apple 共享的系统或商店诊断信息受 Apple 的设置与政策约束。
