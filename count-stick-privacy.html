COUNT STICK

Privacy Policy
隐私政策

Effective date: October 22, 2026 · 生效日期：2026年10月22日

Your photos stay on your device

Count Stick is a photo-based stick and skewer counting app provided by XM LLC. Counting runs on your device. The app does not upload your photos or counting results to XM LLC or an AI provider.

Camera and photo selection

Camera access is used only when you choose to take a photo. You can deny or revoke access in iOS Settings and still import a photo. The system photo picker gives the app access to the images you select, without requiring access to your whole photo library.

Saved history

When you save a count, the app stores the photo, markers, total, date and optional note in its local storage. These records are excluded from the app's iCloud backup. You can delete individual records in History; uninstalling the app removes its local records.

Sharing

Sharing happens only when you choose Share and select a destination in the system share sheet. The selected app or recipient receives what you share under its own privacy practices.

No accounts, advertising or tracking

Count Stick does not require an account and does not include advertising, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. XM LLC does not receive your in-app photos, counts or usage history. Apple processes App Store purchases under Apple's privacy policy. App Store or operating-system diagnostics you elect to share are governed by Apple's settings and policies.

Support and policy changes

If you email us, we receive the message and details you choose to provide, and use them to respond to your request. Please avoid sending sensitive photos. This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the app; the effective date will be updated on this page.

照片留在你的设备上

Count Stick 是 XM LLC 提供的拍照数签工具。计数在设备本地进行，App 不会将照片或计数结果上传给 XM LLC 或 AI 服务提供商。

相机与照片选择

只有在你选择拍照时，App 才会使用相机。你可以拒绝或在 iOS 设置中撤销权限，仍可选择照片。系统照片选择器仅向 App 提供你主动选中的照片，无需开放整个相册。

本地历史记录

当你主动保存计数时，照片、标记、数量、日期和可选备注会保存在 App 的本地存储中。这些记录不纳入 App 的 iCloud 备份。你可以在历史记录中逐条删除；卸载 App 会移除其本地记录。

分享

只有在你点击分享并在系统分享菜单中选择目标后，内容才会发给所选 App 或接收者。对方按其自身隐私规则处理这些内容。

无需账号，无广告和追踪

Count Stick 无需注册账号，不包含广告、第三方分析或追踪 SDK。XM LLC 不会收到 App 内的照片、计数或使用记录。App Store 购买由 Apple 按其隐私政策处理；你选择向 Apple 共享的系统或商店诊断信息受 Apple 的设置与政策约束。

支持与政策更新

如果你通过邮件联系我们，我们会收到你主动提供的内容，并用来回复支持请求。请避免发送敏感照片。如 App 功能发生变化，本政策可能随之更新，页面会注明新的生效日期。
