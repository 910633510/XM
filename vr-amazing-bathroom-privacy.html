Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026. XM LLC provides VR Amazing Bathroom for Meta Quest. This policy covers the immersive app and its optional floating web browser.

Exploring the worlds

You can explore the scenes and play the bundled film offline. The app uses your headset’s position and orientation, tracking origin, controller pose and button or stick movements to draw the view, move your character and operate the floating screen. These inputs are processed on your headset. The app does not send a movement history to the publisher. You do not need an account with the publisher to play.

Opening a website

The browser starts with a page and a film included in the app. When you open a link or submit a web address, Android WebView requests the page from that website. The website and the services that supply its content can receive your IP address, the page requested and information from the browser, such as its type. The site can also receive information you give it, such as text entered in a search box or a sign-in form. If you submit search terms in the app’s address bar, the browser sends them to Baidu. Selecting Paste reads clipboard text into the field you selected.

Links on the home page include CCTV, NASA+, Bilibili and YouTube. You can also enter other websites. The privacy policy of the service you visit governs its handling of your requests, sign-ins and other information you provide. The publisher does not operate a proxy for your traffic, receive the contents of the web pages you view, or collect your website passwords. To show a video without its surrounding page, the app uses the rendered browser image and adjusts the page layout or the displayed region on your headset.

Storage on your headset

Websites may use cookies, local storage and cached files in the app’s WebView on your headset. These may keep you signed in between visits. Closing the floating screen ends playback and closes the page. Stored website data can remain until it expires or is cleared. Sign out of websites after viewing on a shared headset.

Quest and Unity

Meta provides the headset software and store services. Its privacy policy covers information it handles for these services. The app is built with Unity. Unity’s player privacy policy describes the technical device and usage information its software may process. Unity Ads, Unity Analytics and Unity cloud crash reporting are disabled in this Quest build. Meta privacy information · Unity player privacy policy

Access or deletion requests

To request access to or deletion of information held by the publisher, email xm910llc@gmail.com with the subject “VR Amazing Bathroom privacy request”. Tell us which information you want us to locate, such as an earlier support message. You may contact us from any region, free of charge. We use your email address and the information you send to respond. We keep support correspondence for as long as needed to handle the request or meet applicable legal obligations. Please leave passwords and browsing history out of your message.

To remove local website data, close the app and clear the saved data for VR Amazing Bathroom in your headset’s storage settings. If Meta has backed up the app’s data, you can also manage or delete that backup in your Meta account. For information saved by a website or its account service, use that service’s privacy or account controls.

隐私政策

生效日期为 2026 年 11 月 20 日。XM LLC 提供 VR Amazing Bathroom 的 Meta Quest 版本。本政策适用于游戏及其中可选择启用的悬浮浏览器。

探索游戏世界

你可以离线游览场景、播放随游戏附带的短片。游戏在头显上处理头部位置和方向、追踪原点、手柄位置和朝向，以及按键和摇杆输入，用于绘制视野、移动和操作悬浮屏幕。游戏不会把动作轨迹发送给发行方。游玩无需注册发行方账号。

打开外部网站

浏览器的起始页和短片都随游戏提供。打开链接或提交网址时，系统 WebView 会向目标网站请求网页。网站和为其提供内容的服务商可能收到你的 IP 地址、请求的网页，以及浏览器类型等技术信息。网站也可能收到你在搜索框或登录表单中输入的内容。在游戏地址栏提交搜索词时，浏览器会将搜索词发给百度。选择「粘贴」会读取剪贴板文字并填入当前输入框。

首页提供央视直播、NASA+、哔哩哔哩和 YouTube 的入口。你也可以输入其他网址。网站如何处理访问请求、账号登录及你提供的信息，由该服务自己的隐私政策说明。发行方不代理网页流量，不接收你看到的网页内容，也不收集网站密码。无边框播放使用头显上已渲染的网页画面，通过调整网页布局或截取显示区域呈现视频。

头显内的浏览数据

网站可以通过游戏的 WebView 在头显内保存 Cookie、网站本地存储和缓存文件。这些数据可能让登录状态延续到下次访问。关闭悬浮屏幕会停止播放并关闭网页，已保存的网站数据可能保留至到期或清除。在共用头显上观看后，建议退出网站账号。

Quest 和 Unity

Meta 提供头显系统和商店服务，其隐私政策说明这些服务如何处理信息。游戏使用 Unity 引擎，Unity 的玩家隐私政策说明其软件可能处理的设备及使用信息。本 Quest 版本已关闭 Unity 广告、Unity Analytics 及 Unity 云端崩溃报告。Meta 隐私信息 · Unity 玩家隐私政策
