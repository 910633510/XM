Chain Rift 0.2.0 Privacy Policy

November 18, 2026 · XM LLC

Playing on your headset

Chain Rift uses headset and controller tracking to show your view and control your character, blade and hook. You can play solo and complete the tutorial offline. The app saves language, difficulty, turning and comfort settings, your selected hook, tutorial completion and achievement progress in local app storage.

Quick matches and private rooms

When you choose Quick Match, Create Room or Join Room, your headset contacts XM LLC’s room service over HTTPS. The service processes your IP address to receive your connection and limit repeated requests. It uses a random request identifier, a room code, a temporary session token, a player slot and the time of your last activity to admit players, identify retries and detect disconnects.

During a match, the guest’s headset sends in-game position, facing and aim, hook choice and throw strength, swings, targets and upgrade selections. The host’s headset runs the match and sends updates for both players and the bots. These include character positions and movement, facing, hook origin and path, health, knockouts, upgrades, runes, timers, scores and match statistics. In-game movements and directions can reflect how you move your head or controllers. The room service relays the guest’s actions to the host and the host’s updates to the guest so both players can see and play the same match.

How long online information stays

The room service keeps the latest inputs and match update in memory. New updates replace the previous ones. An inactive session times out after about 45 seconds. Once a session closes, the service clears its input and keeps its temporary identifiers for about two minutes to handle retries. The final match update expires about two minutes after the room closes. An IP address used to limit repeated matchmaking or room-entry requests expires from the service’s rate limiter about one minute after the last such request from that address. These periods apply to the room service’s memory. The hosting provider and network systems may also process connection and error information to deliver and secure the service.

Managing and deleting information

Use Leave Room or exit the game to stop sharing match updates. Clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app removes local settings and progress. Backup, restore, Meta accounts, and any headset-level recording or sharing follow your platform settings and Meta’s privacy policy.

If you email XM LLC, we use your email address and the information you provide to respond. For privacy questions or a deletion request, including information you sent to support, email xm910llc@gmail.com.

锁链裂谷 0.2.0 隐私政策

2026 年 11 月 18 日 · XM LLC

在头显上游玩

锁链裂谷使用头显和手柄追踪来呈现视角，并控制角色、刀刃和锁钩。你可以离线游玩单人模式并完成教程。语言、难度、转向、舒适设置、所选锁钩、教程完成情况和成就进度保存在应用的本地存储中。

快速匹配和私人房间

选择快速匹配、创建房间或加入房间时，头显会通过 HTTPS 连接 XM LLC 的房间服务。服务会处理你的 IP 地址以接收连接并限制重复请求。服务使用随机请求标识、房间码、临时会话令牌、玩家席位和最近活动时间，让玩家进入房间、识别重试并检测掉线。

对局中，加入方的头显会发送角色在游戏中的位置、面朝和瞄准方向、所选锁钩、投掷力度、挥刀动作、目标和升级选择。房主的头显运算整场对局，并发送双方玩家和机器人角色的位置、移动和朝向，以及锁钩起点和轨迹、生命值、击倒、升级、符文、计时、比分和对局统计。游戏内的位置和方向可以反映你的头部或手柄移动。房间服务将加入方的动作转发给房主，并将房主生成的对局状态转发给加入方，让双方能在同一场对局中游玩。

联机信息保留时间

房间服务在内存中保留最近的操作和对局状态，每次更新都会替换旧值。会话连续约 45 秒无活动后超时。会话关闭时，服务清除该玩家的操作数据，并保留临时标识约两分钟以处理重试。房间关闭约两分钟后，最终对局状态也会清除。用于限制匹配、创建或加入房间请求频率的 IP 地址记录，会在该地址最后一次发出此类请求约一分钟后从限流记录中清除。这些时间适用于房间服务的内存记录。托管服务商和网络系统也可能处理连接或错误信息，以提供服务和维护安全。

管理和删除信息

离开房间或退出游戏即可停止分享对局更新。清除应用数据或卸载游戏可以移除本地设置和进度。备份、恢复、Meta 账号及头显系统的录制和分享功能遵循平台设置及 Meta 的隐私政策。

如果你给 XM LLC 发邮件，我们会使用你的邮箱地址和你提供的信息来回复。你可通过 xm910llc@gmail.com 咨询隐私问题或请求删除信息，包括你提供给客服的信息。
