## Xsimple Voice Studio for Mac 1.0 (1)

The first direct Mac release brings the Xsimple Voice Studio workspace to a larger canvas, including AI Voices, permission-first Voice Clone, Voice Chat, local playback, and export.

### Install

1. Download `Xsimple-Voice-Studio-1.0.dmg`.
2. Open the disk image and drag **Xsimple Voice Studio** to **Applications**.
3. Launch the app and allow microphone access only when you want to record a reference.

### Compatibility and security

- Requires macOS 15 or later.
- Universal binary for Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
- Signed by XM LLC with Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
- SHA-256: `56ba94086cb65038f2be26ae3b68dbf8f72c06d4f540709c4055bee5e3b6dffe`

Sign in and web billing are being activated together with the account service. iPhone purchases continue to use Apple In-App Purchase.
