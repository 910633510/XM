Privacy notice

Last updated: October 20, 2026

XM LLC provides VR Sports Center. This notice explains the information used to run the game, information exchanged during optional two-player matches, and information you send us when asking for help.

This notice covers VR Sports Center. Meta accounts, the Meta Horizon Store, headset services and other XM LLC products have their own privacy notices.

Information used during play

The game uses your headset and controller positions and orientations, tracking status, and controller inputs to display your view, control equipment and run the sports. It also uses game state such as movement, hits, strokes, scores, race positions and lap times.

Shooting practice, table tennis, golf, tennis, volleyball and kart racing run their gameplay locally on your device. The game does not require you to create a separate in-game account. A platform account may still be needed to obtain or run the game.

Two-player shooting

If you host or join a shooting match, the participating devices connect directly using the host address entered by the joining player. The feature is designed for play on the same local network.

The devices exchange network and session identifiers, head position and orientation, the position and orientation used for your selected shooting hand, tracking status, selected characters, your choice of left or right hand for shooting, and the shots, hits, health, ammunition, scores and other match state needed to play together. The other participant receives the information needed to display your character and synchronize the match.

The game does not provide public matchmaking or a public lobby. Use a network and participants you trust. Direct gameplay traffic is not end-to-end encrypted by the game.

Session state and technical information

The game uses changing state while you play. Ending a shooting session resets its core match and pose state. Your character choices and the last host address can remain in memory during the same app run so you can change modes or reconnect.

The game and headset software can generate technical information about errors and operation. Device and platform logs are separate from the live match state, and ending a match does not necessarily remove those records. If you choose to send us a diagnostic file for help, we handle it as part of your support request.

Support and privacy requests

When you email xm910llc@gmail.com, we receive your email address, sender name if included, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information only to handle your request. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information.

Access to the support mailbox within XM LLC is restricted to the company principal. Email service providers process messages to deliver and store email.

We retain support information for as long as needed to handle your request. After a request is completed, we periodically review and delete information that is no longer needed, except where retention is required by law.

Safety and abuse reports

If you submit a report through Meta's user reporting service, XM LLC may receive and review the report to address safety or abuse in the game. Report information is delivered through Meta's encrypted reporting inbox.

Meta automatically deletes reports from that inbox, including archived reports, within a maximum of 90 days. This retention period applies to the Meta reporting inbox. Information sent separately to our support email is handled under the support policy above.

Purchases and platform sharing

Purchases are handled through the Meta Horizon Store. The game does not ask you to enter payment-card details. Meta handles information for its store, account and headset services under its own notices and settings.

Casting, livestreaming and video recording are available through platform sharing features. If you choose to use them, the platform's sharing controls and privacy notices apply. A recording or broadcast can include gameplay and other participants' on-screen activity.

Your choices and deletion requests

You can play locally without hosting or joining a two-player match. Leaving a network session stops that gameplay connection, and you can close the app at any time. These actions do not delete records held separately by a platform, another participant or an email service.

To ask about, access, correct or delete information you have provided to XM LLC, email xm910llc@gmail.com and identify VR Sports Center. Where possible, write from the address you used for your original message and describe the information or request involved. We may ask for information reasonably needed to identify your request.

We handle requests under the support policy above, including any retention required by law. For information held independently by Meta or another platform, use that provider's privacy controls or support process.

Age and audience

VR Sports Center is intended for people aged 13 and older, or a higher minimum age where required in their region. It is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to XM LLC in connection with the game, contact us so we can review and address the request.

Changes to this notice

We will update this page if the information practices described here change and revise the date above. Please review the current notice when using new features.
