AI Song Cover

AI Song Cover is an iOS music studio app for creating short cover previews from a song file and a user-provided voice reference. Free users can render 30-second previews, while Pro subscribers can render up to 5 minutes.

  • Song and voice files are uploaded over HTTPS to cover.xmaillc.com.
  • Apple StoreKit handles subscription purchases and entitlement proof.
  • Uploaded job files and generated outputs are cleaned up after approximately 24 hours.
