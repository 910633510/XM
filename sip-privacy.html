Sip Privacy Policy

XM LLC

Sip lets you record water by the sip on iPhone and Apple Watch. XM LLC receives no data from the app. Sip uses no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Data on your devices

Sip saves the time and amount of each recorded sip, refill events, cup names and sizes, water levels, daily goals, and reminder preferences. The iPhone app and its Home Screen widget share this information through an Apple app group on the iPhone.

When you use Sip on a paired Apple Watch, Apple WatchConnectivity exchanges cup information, progress, and recording actions with your iPhone. The Watch keeps a local copy of the information it displays and saves pending actions until they sync. Sip uses local sync identifiers to keep a retried action from being recorded twice.

Reminders

Reminders are optional and use Apple’s local notifications. You can turn them off in Sip or change notification permission in iPhone Settings. Sip stores a small local record of scheduling results, such as permission status and the number of reminders scheduled.

Retention and deletion

When Sip records a sip or refill, it removes drink events dated more than 30 full days before that action. Cup settings and the current water level stay on your device while you use the app. You can undo the latest eligible action in Sip. To remove the app’s local data, delete Sip from your iPhone and paired Apple Watch. Apple manages any device backups according to your backup settings.

Contact and support

If you contact XM LLC outside the app, we use the information you choose to send, such as your email address and message, to respond to your request. You can also ask us about privacy or request deletion of support correspondence through the same contact page.

「一口」隐私政策

XM LLC

「一口」让你在 iPhone 和 Apple Watch 上按口记录饮水。XM LLC 不会从 App 接收任何数据。App 无需账号，也没有广告、数据分析或追踪功能。

设备上的数据

「一口」会保存每次喝水的时间和水量、续杯记录、水杯名称和容量、剩余水量、每日目标以及提醒设置。iPhone 上的 App 和主屏幕小组件通过 Apple 的应用组在同一台设备上共享这些信息。

在配对的 Apple Watch 上使用「一口」时，手表会通过 Apple WatchConnectivity 与 iPhone 交换水杯信息、饮水进度和记录操作。手表在本地保存显示所需的数据，并在同步前保留待同步操作。App 使用本地同步标识，避免同一操作因重试被记录两次。

喝水提醒

喝水提醒由你选择开启，使用 Apple 的本地通知。你可以在「一口」中关闭提醒，也可以在 iPhone 的系统设置里修改通知权限。App 在本地保存简要的提醒安排结果，例如权限状态和已安排的提醒数量。

保留与删除

记录喝水或续杯时，App 会清理早于该操作日期 30 个完整自然日的饮水记录。使用 App 期间，水杯设置和剩余水量保存在设备上。你可以在 App 中撤销符合条件的最近一次操作。要清除 App 的本地数据，请从 iPhone 和配对的 Apple Watch 上删除「一口」。设备备份由 Apple 根据你的备份设置管理。

联系与支持

如果你在 App 之外联系 XM LLC，我们会使用你主动提供的信息，例如邮箱地址和留言，来回复和处理你的请求。你也可以通过同一联系页面咨询隐私问题，或请求删除支持往来记录。
