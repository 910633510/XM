简体中文

AI实时计数器在你的 iPhone 上识别人员与车辆，并记录过线或区域通行统计。本应用不要求账号，不向开发者或第三方 AI 服务上传摄像头画面、所选视频或统计记录。

适用范围与联系

本政策说明 XM LLC（AI实时计数器开发者）提供的AI实时计数器 iOS 应用如何处理数据。隐私问题可通过联系页面提出。应用中的在线支持、隐私政策和 Apple 使用条款会打开外部网页；这些网站的处理行为受各自政策约束。

摄像头与所选视频

摄像头权限仅用于你主动开启的实时检测。画面在设备上即时处理，应用不录制摄像头视频，也不使用麦克风。识别模型随应用提供，不需要把画面发送到服务器。

导入使用系统相册选择器，应用只接收你选中的视频。如果视频在 iCloud 中，系统可能需要先下载。用于分析的视频副本保存在应用临时目录；关闭分析页面并停止处理后会尝试删除，异常退出留下的副本会在下次应用启动时清理。应用不会删除相册中的原视频。

保存在设备上的统计

本地记录包括会话标识、开始时间、来源类型、统计时长、完成状态、计数设置（如计数线位置、检测和计数区域、计数模式、A/B 边分组、方向及静止自行车过滤开关）、目标类别、过线或区域通行事件及摄像头统计间断。临时跟踪编号仅用于区分同一统计过程中的目标，不识别个人身份。统计记录不包含原始画面、视频、面部模板或车牌识别结果。

统计记录保存在应用的本地存储中。是否进入操作系统备份取决于你的设备与备份设置；本应用没有开发者运营的云端同步服务。

导出与删除

只有在你选择导出时，CSV 才会交给系统分享或文件保存功能。你选择的接收方或存储服务将按其自身规则处理该副本。开发者不会自动收到导出文件。应用内用于分享的临时 CSV 副本会在下次启动时清理。

在“历史记录”中使用编辑或滑动删除，可删除相应本地会话。卸载应用会删除该安装的本地应用数据。删除会话或卸载应用不会撤回你已导出的副本，也不会删除独立的系统备份；请在相应位置自行管理这些副本。

English

AI Real-Time Counter detects people and vehicles on your iPhone and stores line-crossing or region-transit statistics. No account is required. The app does not upload camera frames, selected videos, or statistics to the developer or third-party AI services.

Scope and contact

This policy explains data handling in the AI Real-Time Counter iOS app provided by XM LLC. For privacy questions, use the contact page. The app's support, privacy, and Apple terms links open external websites. Those websites are governed by their own policies.

Camera and selected videos

Camera access is used for live detection that you start. Frames are processed on your device; the app does not record camera video or use the microphone. The detection model is included with the app, so frames do not need to be sent to a server.

Video import uses the system Photos picker and receives only the video you select. The system may first download an item stored in iCloud. An analysis copy is stored in the app's temporary directory. The app attempts to delete it after the analysis screen closes and processing stops; abandoned copies after an unexpected exit are cleared on the next app launch. The original video in Photos is not deleted.

Statistics stored on your device

Local records include a session identifier, start time, source type, duration, completion state, counting settings (such as line position, detection and counting areas, counting mode, A/B edge groups, direction, and the stationary-bicycle filter setting), object categories, line-crossing or region-transit events, and camera interruptions. Temporary tracking numbers distinguish objects within a counting session; they do not identify people. Statistics do not contain original frames, video, face templates, or license-plate recognition results.

Records remain in the app's local storage. Inclusion in operating-system backups depends on your device and backup settings. The app has no developer-operated cloud synchronization service.

Export and deletion

A CSV is passed to the system share sheet or file-saving interface only when you choose to export it. Your chosen recipient or storage service handles that copy under its own terms. The developer does not automatically receive exports. Temporary CSV sharing copies within the app are cleared on the next launch.

Use Edit or swipe to delete a session in History. Uninstalling the app removes that installation's local app data. Deleting a session or uninstalling does not recall exported copies or remove independent system backups; manage those copies in their respective locations.

Purchase, tracking, and support

Versions 1.0.4 and earlier were offered as paid App Store downloads, with a period of free availability. Version 1.0.5 introduces an Apple auto-renewable monthly subscription with a 3-day free trial for eligible new subscribers. After the trial, the subscription renews monthly at the price shown in Apple’s confirmation unless canceled. Eligible earlier app acquisitions retain access. Saved history and CSV export are not locked when a subscription expires. Refer to the App Store for the available version and current download price.

From version 1.0.5, StoreKit verifies Apple-signed app acquisition and subscription records on the device to determine subscription validity, revocation and earlier purchase access. These purchase records are not sent to the developer. Apple handles payment, subscription management and purchase restoration; the app does not receive payment-card details or payment-account credentials.

To protect customers during the business-model change, version 1.0.5 may automatically download a signed public access policy from www.xmaillc.com and cache the verified policy locally. The request does not include your Apple Account, transaction identifier, purchase record, videos or statistics. The website host, GitHub Pages, processes standard network-request information such as IP addresses under its privacy statement to deliver and protect the service. We do not use this request for advertising or user tracking. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Purchasing, restoring or initially verifying access may require a connection. If an earlier purchase cannot be verified, the app asks you to retry instead of requiring another purchase.

Counting and video analysis run locally on the device without an internet connection. Buying or redownloading the app, retrieving an iCloud video, and opening online support pages may require the respective system or website to connect to the internet.

The app includes no advertising, analytics, tracking, or developer crash-reporting service. If you contact support, the contact information and issue details you choose to provide are used to address that request. Please do not send private footage of other people, payment details, or passwords. Your device model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce the issue are usually sufficient.
