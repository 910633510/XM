AI Song Cover Privacy Policy

This policy explains how XM LLC handles audio files, subscription entitlement proof, and operational data for AI Song Cover.

Jump to Policy Terms of Use

Effective Date: May 13, 2026

Information we collect

  • Song audio files that you import and choose to upload.
  • Voice reference audio that you record or import and choose to upload.
  • Generated cover, vocals, and instrumental output files for each job.
  • Subscription entitlement proof from Apple StoreKit when you use Pro features.
  • Basic operational data such as job status, timestamps, error state, app version, and service logs.

How we use information

  • Generate the AI cover job requested by you.
  • Apply Free and Pro rendering limits, including the 30-second Free preview and up to 5-minute Pro render.
  • Let you download and play generated results after processing completes.
  • Maintain service reliability, troubleshoot failures, and prevent abuse.

Subscriptions and payments

Purchases are handled by Apple through StoreKit. XM LLC does not receive or store your credit card number. The app may send Apple-signed subscription entitlement proof to our backend so we can verify Pro access for longer renders.

Data sharing

XM LLC does not sell personal data, does not broker user audio, and does not use advertising trackers. Audio jobs are processed by XM LLC systems used to operate AI Song Cover. We may disclose information only when required by law or to protect the service from misuse.

Retention and deletion

Uploaded audio files and generated outputs are temporary job artifacts. They are automatically cleaned up after approximately 24 hours. You can also delete downloaded result files from your device at any time.

Security

The app uses HTTPS for uploads and downloads through cover.xmaillc.com. Access to the processing service is limited by backend controls, subscription entitlement verification, and operational safeguards.

Children's privacy

AI Song Cover is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum age required in your region. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

  • You choose which song and voice files to upload.
  • You can deny or revoke microphone access in iOS Settings.
  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple account.
  • You can contact us with privacy or deletion questions.

Contact us

For privacy questions, email privacy@xm-llc.com or contact XM LLC, 554 Boston Post Rd, Milford, CT 06460.
