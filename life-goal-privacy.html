Privacy Policy

xsimple Life Goal Privacy Policy

This policy explains how XM LLC handles information for xsimple Life Goal, localized in mainland China as 人生小目标. The app is built for offline goal planning, with optional on-device AI.

Jump to Policy Support Page

Effective: November 6, 2026

Information Stored in the App

  • Goal descriptions, target dates, daily checklist items, and completion history are stored locally. Device backups may include this data depending on your backup settings.
  • Optional model files are stored on the device and excluded from device backups.

Local AI Processing

  • The built-in planner and daily checklist work offline without a model download or an account.
  • If you choose to download a model, AI planning runs on your device. The app does not send goal descriptions, target dates, checklists, or generated plans to XM LLC or to an AI service.
  • XM LLC does not use your goal content to train AI models.

Network Use

  • Before an optional model download starts, the app shows the approximate download size and lets you continue without the model.
  • When you choose to download a model, the app retrieves the file over HTTPS from Hugging Face and its content delivery network. The request does not include goal content or require a Hugging Face account.
  • The download provider may log your IP address and standard request metadata to operate and protect its service. See the Hugging Face Privacy Policy.

Purchases

  • Paid app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store.
  • XM LLC does not receive or store full payment card numbers.
  • Apple may provide purchase and storefront information needed to operate the App Store purchase.

Data Sharing

  • XM LLC does not sell personal information from xsimple Life Goal.
  • The app does not use advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • XM LLC may disclose information it holds when required by law or to protect users or the service.

Retention and Choices

  • You can decline the model download and keep using the built-in planner. If you previously enabled AI, you can switch back to the built-in planner.
  • Removing the app deletes its local storage, including goals and model files. Manage any device backups separately in your device settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, or support, email privacy@xm-llc.com or contact XM LLC Support.
