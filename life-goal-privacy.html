Privacy Policy

xsimple Life Goal Privacy Policy

This policy explains how XM LLC handles information for xsimple Life Goal, localized in mainland China as 人生小目标. The app is built for private, on-device AI planning.

Jump to Policy Support Page

Effective: May 30, 2026

Information Stored in the App

  • Goal descriptions entered by the user.
  • Target dates, daily checklist items, completion state, and rescaled work.
  • Downloaded local model files needed for offline AI planning.

Local AI Processing

  • AI planning is performed locally on the user's device after the model is downloaded.
  • Goal descriptions and generated plans are not submitted to third-party AI services.
  • XM LLC does not use user goal content to train third-party models.

Network Use

  • The app may download an open-source mobile language model from XM LLC-hosted infrastructure.
  • The download request may include standard network metadata such as IP address and user agent.
  • The app does not upload goal text, checklist activity, or generated plans to XM LLC servers.

Purchases

  • Paid app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store.
  • XM LLC does not receive or store full payment card numbers.
  • Apple may provide purchase and storefront information needed to operate the App Store purchase.

Data Sharing

  • XM LLC does not sell personal information from xsimple Life Goal.
  • XM LLC does not share goal content with third-party AI providers.
  • Information may be disclosed only when required by law or to protect the service and users.

Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, or support, email privacy@xm-llc.com or support@xm-llc.com.
