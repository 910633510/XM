How do purchases work on each platform?

iPhone subscriptions are purchased through Apple. The direct Mac app opens Stripe's secure hosted Checkout. Xsimple Voice Studio never asks you to type card details inside the app.

Can I use an iPhone subscription on Mac?

Yes. Sign in with Apple on both devices. Once the purchase is linked and verified, the active plan is available to the same account.

Where are my recordings stored?

The first Mac release keeps reference recordings and generated files on the device. Audio is sent to XM LLC's processing service only when you ask the app to generate speech.

Can I cancel or delete my account?

You can open subscription management for the provider that billed you and request account deletion from Settings. Deleting an account does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription.

Where can I read the privacy policy?

Read the existing Xsimple Voice Studio privacy policy and contact XM LLC if you have a question.
